Group Coaching — Live Flow

Follow this while you're actually running a session. For the full study reference (why it works, diagnostics, edge cases), use the Cheat Sheet. This is only: where to go next, what to say, and where you're most likely to slip into intellectual coaching.

The one rule underneath every step Find the charge. Don't talk about it. If you're asking "tell me more" or "why" — stop.
  1. 1
    Open
    Get in fast. No catch-up.
    "If I could snap my fingers and make this disappear right now, what's going on for you?"

    Skip "how's it been going." Straight in.

  2. 2
    Problem
    Name it. Don't dig for detail.
    "What's the actual problem right now?"

    You need the shape of it, not the full story. Move on as soon as you have it.

  3. 3
    Pattern
    Find the repeat. Where else does this show up?
    "When this happens, what do you find yourself doing? Where else does this show up in your life?"

    Reflect their own words back open-ended. Don't name the root for them — ask if it's familiar and let them confirm it.

  4. !
    Guardrail — the charge just showed up
    Stop. This is the moment coaches lose it.
    "Stay here. Feel this. What's coming up?"
    Do not ask "tell me more about that." That's the intellectual bypass. Get the feeling's location, temperature, texture. Let eyes close if it helps them go deeper instead of talking their way out.
  5. 4
    Possibilities
    Wait for it. Don't hand it over.
    "If this pattern wasn't running the show — what's possible? What's this here to teach you?"
    Silence is the work. Don't fill it. If they surface the answer themselves, that's the real shift. Only give it to them if they truly can't find it — and know that's the smaller win, not the goal.
  6. 5
    Plan
    One thing. Checkable.
    "What's the one thing you'll actually do about this before we talk again?"

    One action, not a list. Specific enough you could ask them next time whether they did it.

  7. 6
    Close
    Recap in one line. Move on.
    "So: [problem] → [pattern] → what you're taking → [plan]."

    Keep it tight. Next volunteer.

When to stop using this page When Step 3 → Guardrail → Step 4 happens without you thinking about it. That's the whole guide, internalised.
